Manali
Manali is a scenic hill station nestled in the Beas River Valley of Himachal Pradesh
, famous for its snow-capped mountains, apple orchards, and outdoor adventure activities.
Goa
Goa is India's smallest state, famous for its golden sandy beaches, rich Portuguese
colonial history, and vibrant coastal culture.
Jaipur
Jaipur, known as the "Pink City," is the vibrant capital and largest city of Rajasthan
, India, famous for its historic palaces, forts, and unique rose-colored architecture.
