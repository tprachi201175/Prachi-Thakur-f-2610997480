Popular Destinations in India

Agra is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India, famed globally for housing the Taj Mahal, a magnificent white marble mausoleum designated as one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Located on the banks of the Yamuna River in Uttar Pradesh, this historic city draws millions of international and domestic travelers each year who come to witness the breathtaking Mughal architecture of the monument alongside other UNESCO World Heritage sites like Agra Fort and the nearby ghost city of Fatehpur Sikri.
