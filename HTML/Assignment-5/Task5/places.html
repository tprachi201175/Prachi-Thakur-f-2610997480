Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan for his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Golden Temple

The holiest shrine in Sikhism, also known as Sri Harmandir Sahib.Covered in real gold leaf and surrounded by a holy pool of water called the Amrit Sarovar.

Hawa Mahal

A tall, pink sandstone palace known as the "Palace of Winds". It has 953 small windows called jharokhas designed to let cool air flow through the building.

Jaipur

The capital city of Rajasthan, widely known as the "Pink City" due to the distinct color of its historic buildings. It houses majestic royal residences like the hilltop Amer.
Red Fort
The Red Fort is a massive historic fort made of red sandstone.Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan built it when he moved his capital to Delhi.
